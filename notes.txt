details of mechanics:
	
	"Cards" are the things that make up your deck. They cost energy to play and put "Items" on the scale when played
	"Items" have a weight and their own effects
	
	Cards and Items will typically come in pairs, but not always
	
	if the effects are simple, then the Card and Item can share a title
	
	example pair:
		Card:
			title: Ice Cube
			cost: 2
			description: Place an Ice Cube on the scale
			rarity: common
		Item:
			title: Ice Cube
			weight: 3
			effect: -1 weight each time you play a card
	
	Cards dont need to spawn anything, they can do other stuff too
	
	Cards don't respond to stuff, Items do
